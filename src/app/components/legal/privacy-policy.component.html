Trivimind is a trivia game. This policy describes what information the app handles, why it handles it, and who else sees it. Everything below about data collection was written by reading the application's own source code, so it describes what the app actually does rather than what a generic policy template assumes.

Information you give us

Playing without an account

The moment you open Trivimind, before you interact with anything, the app creates an anonymous account for you. It is a random identifier with no personal information attached to it. It exists so you can play, and so your session persists if you close the tab and come back. You are never asked to sign in to play.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. It is only needed to save a score to the public leaderboard, or to subscribe to Pro. If you sign in from an anonymous session, we upgrade that same account rather than creating a second one, so anything already saved carries over.

  • Email and password. We store your email address. Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and never passes through our own code. We also store whether your email address has been verified, because saving a score requires a verified address.
  • Google, Facebook, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple, X/Twitter or Yahoo. The provider gives us your email address, your display name, and an account identifier. We request nothing beyond that, and we never receive your password. Your browser only contacts the provider at the moment you click one of these buttons — not on page load.
  • Display name. Optional and set by you. If you save a score, this name is shown publicly on the leaderboard.

Questions you contribute

Pro subscribers can add questions to the shared question bank. The question text, the answers, the category and the difficulty are published publicly and shown to other players. Treat anything you submit as public. Contributed questions cannot currently be edited or withdrawn from within the app.

Information created by using the app

Leaderboard entries — these are public

When you save a score, we store your display name, the score, how many questions the game had, the resulting percentage, and when you saved it. The leaderboard is readable by anyone, including people who are not signed in. We keep one entry per account, holding your best score.

Subscription records

If you subscribe to Pro, we store the status of your subscription, which plan and price it is for, and whether it is set to cancel at the end of the period. This does not include any card details — see below.

Payments

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see, receive or store your card number. When you first subscribe, we send Stripe your email address and an internal account identifier so your subscription can be matched to your Trivimind account. Everything after that — the payment form, the billing portal, your saved payment methods — happens on Stripe's own systems under Stripe's privacy policy.

What is stored on your device

Trivimind sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage instead:

  • Local storage — your light/dark theme preference, and your sign-in session (managed by Firebase Authentication).
  • IndexedDB — up to 200 trivia questions you have already been served, so a game can start with no network connection. This contains no personal information.
  • Service worker cache — the app's own files (scripts, styles, icons), so it loads offline and can be installed to your home screen.

All of it is removed if you clear site data for this site in your browser.

Who else your browser contacts

Using Trivimind necessarily reveals your IP address to the services below, as it would with any website:

Service What it does When
Google Firebase Hosting, sign-in, database and server functions — the app's entire backend Always
Open Trivia DB Supplies the general trivia questions When questions are fetched
Google Fonts Serves the Inter typeface Always
Stripe Payment and billing management Only if you subscribe or manage billing
Your chosen sign-in provider Authenticates you Only when you click that provider

The Google Fonts row is a live issue, not just a disclosure. Because the typeface is loaded from Google's servers rather than our own, every visitor's IP address is sent to Google before any consent is obtained. A German court (LG München I, case 3 O 17493/20) held that this specific arrangement breaches the GDPR. Serving the font from our own domain removes the transfer entirely and also loads faster. If that change ships, delete this row and this notice.

What we do not do

Trivimind has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party marketing tools of any kind. We do not sell or share your information, and we do not build a profile of you. The services in the table above are there because the app cannot function without them, not to observe you.

How long we keep it

Needs a decision, and the honest current answer is uncomfortable: there is no automated deletion of anything. Anonymous accounts (one per visitor, plus another each time someone signs out), leaderboard entries, contributed questions and checkout records are all retained indefinitely. A retention period has to be chosen for each, and the deletion mechanism has to be built, before this section can make any promise.
