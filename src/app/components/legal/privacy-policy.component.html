Trivimind is a trivia game. This policy describes what information the app handles, why it handles it, and who else sees it. Everything below about data was written by reading the application's own source code, so it describes what the app actually does rather than what a generic policy template assumes.

The short version: you can play without an account, the app has no analytics or advertising of any kind, it sets no cookies, and nothing about you is sold or shared. The leaderboard and the community question bank are public — everything else is not.

Information you give us

Playing without an account

The moment you open Trivimind, before you interact with anything, the app creates an anonymous account for you. It is a random identifier with no personal information attached to it. It exists so you can play and so your session survives closing the tab. You are never asked to sign in to play.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. It is only needed to save a score to the public leaderboard, or to subscribe to Pro. If you sign in from an anonymous session, that same account is upgraded rather than a second one being created, so anything already saved carries over.

  • Email and password. We store your email address, and whether it has been verified — saving a score requires a verified address. Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and never passes through our own code. If you ask for a password reset, the reset email is sent by Firebase; we are told nothing about whether you opened it.
  • Google, Facebook, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple, Twitter/X or Yahoo. The provider gives us your email address, your display name, and an account identifier. We request nothing beyond that and never receive your password. Your browser only contacts the provider at the moment you click one of these buttons — not on page load.
  • Display name. Optional and set by you. If you save a score, this name is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so choose it accordingly.

Questions you contribute

Pro subscribers can add questions to the shared question bank. The question text, the answers, the category and the difficulty are published publicly and shown to every other player. Your account identifier is stored alongside each question as its author. Treat anything you submit as permanently public, and please don't put personal information about yourself or anyone else into a question — see the Terms for what happens to contributions if you later delete your account.

Submissions are published immediately, without review. There is currently no way to edit or withdraw a question from within the app; to have one removed, write to the address above.

Reports you file about a question

Any signed-in player can flag a question and file a report. The report stores the question's identifier, the reason you chose, anything you typed in the optional detail box, and your account identifier. Reports are readable only by us — never by other players, and not through the app at all.

Information created by using the app

Leaderboard entries — these are public

When you save a score we store your display name, the score, how many questions the game had, the resulting percentage, and when you saved it. The leaderboards are readable by anyone, including people who are not signed in. There is a separate board for each time limit — 15 seconds, 30 seconds and no limit — so you may hold up to one entry on each, and each keeps only your best score.

Subscription records

If you subscribe to Pro we store the status of your subscription, which plan and price it is for, and whether it is set to cancel at the end of the period. This includes no card details — see below.

Why we handle it, and on what basis

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or Brazil's LGPD apply to you, these are the grounds we rely on. We do not ask for consent for any of it, because none of it is the kind of processing that consent would be the right basis for — there is no advertising, profiling or tracking here to consent to.

What we do Why Basis
Create an account and keep you signed in The app cannot run a game or save anything without one Performance of a contract
Store and publish leaderboard entries You asked us to publish that score Performance of a contract
Take payment and manage a Pro subscription Delivering the subscription you bought Performance of a contract

No automated decision-making and no profiling. Nothing here makes a decision about you automatically, and no profile of you is built.

Payments

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see, receive or store your card number. When you first subscribe we send Stripe your email address and an internal account identifier, so your subscription can be matched to your Trivimind account. Everything after that — the payment form, the billing portal, your saved payment methods — happens on Stripe's own systems under Stripe's privacy policy.

What is stored on your device

Trivimind sets no cookies. It uses your browser's own storage instead:

  • Local storage — your light/dark theme preference, and your sign-in session (managed by Firebase Authentication).
  • IndexedDB — up to 200 trivia questions you have already been served, so a game can start with no network connection; and a snapshot of a game in progress, so a reload or a crash doesn't lose it. The saved game is discarded after six hours. Neither contains personal information beyond the game itself.
  • Service worker cache — the app's own files (scripts, styles, icons), so it loads offline and can be installed to your home screen.

All of it is removed if you clear site data for this site in your browser.

Who else your browser contacts

Using Trivimind necessarily reveals your IP address to the services below, as it would with any website. There are no others: the app declares an explicit allow-list of every host it may talk to, enforced by the browser.

Service What it does When

Open Trivia DB Supplies the general trivia questions. We send it no information about you When questions are fetched
Stripe Payment and billing management Only if you subscribe or manage billing
Your chosen sign-in provider Authenticates you Only at the moment you click that provider

Fonts, scripts, styles and images are all served from Trivimind's own domain. Nothing is loaded from a third-party CDN, so no font or asset request tells anyone else that you visited.

What we do not do

Trivimind has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party marketing tools of any kind. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not build a profile of you. The services in the table above are there because the app cannot function without them, not to observe you.

Where your information is stored

The database is hosted in Google Cloud's nam5 multi-region, in the United States. Firebase Authentication and the server functions are operated by Google and may process data in the United States as well. Stripe processes payment data on its own infrastructure, which is also primarily in the United States.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Brazil, this means your information is transferred outside your country. Those transfers rely on the data-processing terms and standard contractual clauses we have in place with Google and Stripe as our processors. You can ask us for details of these at the contact address above.

How long we keep it

We would rather describe this accurately than quote a period we don't actually enforce. What is true today:

  • Your account, leaderboard entries and billing records are kept for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and they go — see below.
  • Checkout and billing-portal records expire 24 hours after they are created and are then deleted automatically. The sweep is best-effort, so treat 24 hours as the earliest they go, not a guarantee of the latest.
  • A game saved in your browser is discarded after six hours; the offline question pool is capped at 200 questions, oldest first.
  • Questions you contributed stay in the shared bank indefinitely, including after you delete your account, with your authorship removed. This is the one place where deletion is not complete, and it is deliberate — the reasoning is in the next section and in the Terms.
  • Reports you file about a question are kept indefinitely, including your account identifier, and are not removed when you delete your account. A report is a record that something needed looking at; if it vanished when its author left, the abuse signal would go with it.
  • Anonymous accounts that are never signed in to hold no personal information, and no data of any kind is attached to them. Firebase deletes them automatically after 30 days without activity.

Your rights over your information

Two of the important ones are built into the app, in the account menu, and need no request to us:

  • Download my data gives you a JSON file containing your account details, your leaderboard entries on every board, every question you have contributed, and your billing records. It also lists what is deliberately not in it and why.
  • Delete account cancels any live subscription, deletes your Stripe customer record and the payment methods stored against it, deletes your leaderboard entries on every board, deletes your billing records, and deletes your sign-in account itself. Questions you contributed are kept, with your authorship replaced by a [deleted-user] marker. They are part of a bank other players are drawing on; what goes is the link between them and you. This is irreversible, and there is no recovery period.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to ask us to correct information, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, or to complain to a data protection authority — in Brazil, the ANPD; in the EEA or UK, your national authority. To exercise any of these, write to quizloop.trivia@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 15 days, which is the shortest deadline that applies to us.

You do not have to give us anything beyond an account to play. An email address is only required if you want to save a score or subscribe.

Children

Trivimind is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. You must be 13 or older to use it, as set out in the Terms. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and there is no age verification — the app asks for a date of birth nowhere.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to quizloop.trivia@gmail.com and we will delete it and its data.

How your information is protected

Rather than describe this in adjectives, here is what is actually in place. Everything is served over HTTPS only. Access to every record in the database is enforced by server-side rules, not by the app's interface, so a request that bypasses the app entirely is refused the same way — your billing records, for instance, are readable only by you, and reports are readable by nobody through the app. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never reach our own code. Payment card details never reach us at all. The app sends a strict Content Security Policy that names every host the browser is allowed to contact and forbids being embedded in a frame by anyone.

No system is perfectly secure, and we don't claim otherwise. If you find a security problem, please tell us at quizloop.trivia@gmail.com before disclosing it publicly.
