The service

Questions you contribute

Questions submitted by Pro subscribers are added to a shared, publicly readable bank and shown to other players. There is currently no way to edit or withdraw a submitted question from within the app, and submissions are published immediately without review.

Pro subscription

Pro costs $0.99 per month and unlocks the ability to contribute questions to the shared bank. Everything else — unlimited games and the leaderboard — is free and stays free.

  • Payment is taken by Stripe. We never see your card details.
  • The subscription renews monthly until cancelled.
  • You can cancel at any time through the billing portal, reachable from the account menu. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues to the end of the period already paid for.
  • If the price changes, the change applies from your next renewal — the app reads the current price live rather than having it fixed in the application.
