Questions you contribute

Questions submitted by Pro subscribers are added to a shared, publicly readable bank and shown to other players. Submissions are reviewed first: a question you submit is not served in anyone's game until a reviewer approves it, and we do not promise how long that will take. We may decline to approve a question, or stop serving one that was previously approved, for any reason — including that it is inaccurate, unclear, duplicated or breaks the rules below. Declining a question does not entitle you to a refund: the subscription pays for the ability to contribute, not for a guarantee that every contribution is published.

Editing and removing a question you contributed

Every question you have contributed is listed on Your questions in the account menu, with what became of it: pending, approved, or rejected with the reviewer's reason where one was given. From there you can do two things, and neither needs a current Pro subscription — the subscription pays for contributing more questions, not for the right to correct the ones you have already written.

  • Edit it. The question goes back to pending and is not served again until a reviewer approves the new version.
  • Remove it from the app. It stops being served in games straight away, and it leaves the reviewers' queue.

Removing a question is not the same as unpublishing it everywhere, and it is worth being plain about the difference:

  • It does not revoke the licence you granted when you contributed it. That licence is irrevocable — see below — and it is what allows anything already built on the question to keep working.
  • It cannot reach copies already served: a question that has been played is on the players' screens, a question saved for offline play is in their browsers, and a question included in a data export somebody has already downloaded is in that file.

If a question needs to come out of circulation for a legal reason rather than because you changed your mind — it is unlawful, it infringes someone's rights, or it contains personal information about a real person — write to quizloop.trivia@gmail.com instead. That route is unchanged, and it is the one that can reach what removing a question from the app cannot.

What happens to your questions if you delete your account

This is the part worth reading twice, because it is the one place where deleting your account does not delete everything.

Questions you contributed stay in the shared bank. Your authorship is removed from them. Everything else goes — your sign-in account, your leaderboard entries, your billing records — but the questions themselves remain publicly visible, with the record of who wrote them replaced by a [deleted-user] marker.

The reason is that the bank is shared. Other players are drawing questions from it as they play, and one prolific contributor leaving would empty whole categories for everyone else. So what is erased on your request is the link between those questions and you, not the contribution the rest of the game depends on. Removing your name is part of fulfilling your deletion request, not something done to you against your wishes.

The two are separate acts and can be combined: if you want particular questions out of play as well, remove them from Your questions before you delete your account. Deletion itself leaves whatever is still in the bank there, and once the account is gone there is nobody the app will accept a removal from — a question marked [deleted-user] can no longer be edited or withdrawn by anyone.

If a specific question needs to come out of the bank entirely — because it is unlawful, infringes someone's rights, or contains personal information about a real person — write to quizloop.trivia@gmail.com and we will remove it. Any player can also flag a question while playing it and file a report from the game-over screen.
